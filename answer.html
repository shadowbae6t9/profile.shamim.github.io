PART A — GRAMMAR

Marks: 60

Topic: Articles

1. ARTICLES — 5 Marks

(a) Bangladesh is ___ independent country.
✓ Correct Answer
an
✗ Common Wrong Answer
a independent
💡 Why?
“Independent” begins with a vowel sound, so we use an, not a.
(b) He is ___ honest student.
✓ Correct Answer
an
✗ Common Wrong Answer
a honest
💡 Why?
The h in “honest” is silent. The word begins with a vowel sound, so we use an.
(c) ___ sun rises in the east.
✓ Correct Answer
The
✗ Common Wrong Answer
A sun
💡 Why?
“Sun” refers to the unique star of our solar system. Unique objects normally take the.
(d) She bought ___ umbrella yesterday.
✓ Correct Answer
an
✗ Common Wrong Answer
a umbrella
💡 Why?
“Umbrella” begins with a vowel sound, so an is used.
(e) He is ___ European.
✓ Correct Answer
a
✗ Common Wrong Answer
an European
💡 Why?
“European” begins with the consonant sound /juː/, like “you”. Therefore, we use a.
Marking: 1 mark each.
Topic: Prepositions

2. PREPOSITIONS — 5 Marks

(a) He is good ___ English.
✓ Correct Answer
at
✗ Common Wrong Answer
in
💡 Why?
The standard expression is good at something.
(b) He is interested ___ learning English.
✓ Correct Answer
in
✗ Common Wrong Answer
at
💡 Why?
The correct expression is interested in + noun/gerund.
(c) He has been absent ___ Monday.
✓ Correct Answer
since
✗ Common Wrong Answer
for
💡 Why?
Since is used with a specific starting point in time, such as Monday.
(d) He was accused ___ theft.
✓ Correct Answer
of
✗ Common Wrong Answer
for
💡 Why?
The standard expression is accused of something.
(e) He is indifferent ___ our duties.
✓ Correct Answer
to
✗ Common Wrong Answer
with
💡 Why?
The standard expression is indifferent to something.
Marking: 1 mark each.
Topic: Special Uses of Words/Phrases

3. SPECIAL USES OF WORDS/PHRASES — 5 Marks

(a) I would rather ___ at home than go outside.
✓ Correct Answer
stay
✗ Common Wrong Answer
staying
💡 Why?
The structure is would rather + base verb + than + base verb.
(b) You had better ___ the truth.
✓ Correct Answer
tell
✗ Common Wrong Answer
to tell
💡 Why?
After had better, we use the base form of the verb without “to”.
(c) He cannot ___ a pen, let alone buy a laptop.
✓ Correct Answer
buy
✗ Common Wrong Answer
buys
💡 Why?
After the modal auxiliary cannot, the base form buy is required.
(d) Walk carefully lest you ___ fall.
✓ Correct Answer
should
✗ Common Wrong Answer
will
💡 Why?
A common examination structure is lest + subject + should + base verb.
(e) No sooner had he reached home ___ it started raining.
✓ Correct Answer
than
✗ Common Wrong Answer
when
💡 Why?
The fixed correlative structure is No sooner ... than ....
Marking: 1 mark each.
Topic: Completing Sentences

4. COMPLETING SENTENCES — 5 Marks

(a) It is high time we ___.
✓ Correct Answer
took action.
✗ Common Wrong Answer
take action.
💡 Why?
After It is high time, the past form is commonly used to express that something should already have been done.
(b) I wish I ___.
✓ Correct Answer
were a bird.
✗ Common Wrong Answer
am a bird.
💡 Why?
For an unreal or hypothetical wish, the subjunctive were is used.
(c) If I were a bird, I ___.
✓ Correct Answer
would fly in the sky.
✗ Common Wrong Answer
will fly in the sky.
💡 Why?
This is a second conditional sentence: If + past form, ... would + base verb.
(d) Unless you work hard, ___.
✓ Correct Answer
you will fail.
✗ Common Wrong Answer
you would fail.
💡 Why?
Unless means “if ... not”. A real future condition commonly takes present simple + will.
(e) He talks as if he ___.
✓ Correct Answer
knew everything.
✗ Common Wrong Answer
knows everything.
💡 Why?
As if can be followed by a past form when the comparison is unreal or contrary to fact.
Important: Other grammatically correct and meaningful completions may also be accepted.
Marking: 1 mark each.
Topic: Right Form of Verbs

5. RIGHT FORM OF VERBS — 5 Marks

(a) The sun ___ in the east.
✓ Correct Answer
rises
✗ Common Wrong Answer
is rising
💡 Why?
A universal or habitual truth takes the simple present tense.
(b) He ___ a book now.
✓ Correct Answer
is reading
✗ Common Wrong Answer
reads
💡 Why?
“Now” indicates an action happening at the present moment, so the present continuous is appropriate.
(c) He ___ here since 2020.
✓ Correct Answer
has been living
✗ Common Wrong Answer
lived
💡 Why?
“Since 2020” indicates an action that started in the past and continues to the present.
(d) When I reached the station, the train ___.
✓ Correct Answer
had left
✗ Common Wrong Answer
left
💡 Why?
The train left before I reached the station, so the earlier past action takes the past perfect.
(e) Had I known the matter, I ___ him.
✓ Correct Answer
would have helped
✗ Common Wrong Answer
would help
💡 Why?
This is a third conditional structure: Had + past participle ... would have + past participle.
Marking: 1 mark each.
Topic: Transformation of Sentences

6. TRANSFORMATION OF SENTENCES — 5 Marks

(a) Everybody loves flowers.
✓ Correct Transformation
There is nobody who does not love flowers.
✗ Wrong Example
There is nobody who loves flowers.
💡 Why?
“Everybody loves flowers” means there is no person who does not love flowers. Removing does not changes the meaning.
(b) He is too weak to walk.
✓ Correct Transformation
He is so weak that he cannot walk.
✗ Wrong Example
He is so weak that he can walk.
💡 Why?
“Too weak to walk” means he is unable to walk. Therefore, cannot walk is required.
(c) Though he is poor, he is honest.
✓ Correct Transformation
He is poor but he is honest.
✗ Wrong Example
He is poor and he is honest.
💡 Why?
“Though” shows contrast, so the transformation should preserve the contrast using but.
(d) Dhaka is one of the largest cities in Bangladesh.
✓ Correct Transformation
Very few cities in Bangladesh are as large as Dhaka.
✗ Wrong Example
All cities in Bangladesh are as large as Dhaka.
💡 Why?
“One of the largest” means only a small number of cities are as large as Dhaka. “All cities” changes the meaning.
(e) He wrote a letter.
✓ Correct Transformation
A letter was written by him.
✗ Wrong Example
A letter is written by him.
💡 Why?
The original sentence is in the past simple tense, so the passive form must also remain in the past: was written.
Marking: 1 mark each.
Topic: Narration

7. NARRATION — 5 Marks

(a) He said, “I am busy.”
✓ Correct Narration
He said that he was busy.
✗ Common Wrong Answer
He said that I am busy.
💡 Why?
“I” changes to “he”, and “am” normally changes to was after the past reporting verb.
(b) He said to me, “Are you happy?”
✓ Correct Narration
He asked me if/whether I was happy.
✗ Common Wrong Answer
He told me that I was happy.
💡 Why?
The direct speech is an interrogative question. Therefore, asked and if/whether are appropriate.
(c) The teacher said to the students, “Do not make a noise.”
✓ Correct Narration
The teacher forbade the students to make a noise.
✗ Common Wrong Answer
The teacher told the students to not make a noise.
💡 Why?
A negative command can be reported using forbade + object + to + verb.
(d) Mother said to me, “May Allah bless you.”
✓ Correct Narration
Mother prayed that Allah might bless me.
✗ Common Wrong Answer
Mother asked me that Allah might bless me.
💡 Why?
The sentence expresses a prayer or wish, so prayed is appropriate.
(e) He said, “What a beautiful flower it is!”
✓ Correct Narration
He exclaimed with wonder that it was a very beautiful flower.
✗ Common Wrong Answer
He asked that it was a beautiful flower.
💡 Why?
The direct speech is an exclamatory sentence expressing wonder, so exclaimed with wonder is used.
Marking: 1 mark each.
Topic: Pronoun Reference

8. PRONOUN REFERENCE — 5 Marks

Passage:

Trees are very useful to us. They give us oxygen and protect the environment. Without them, human life would be impossible. We should therefore plant more trees and take care of them.
(a) “They” refers to —
✓ Correct Answer
trees
✗ Wrong Answer
oxygen
💡 Why?
Trees are the subject that gives oxygen and protects the environment. Therefore, “they” refers to trees.
(b) “Them” in “Without them” refers to —
✓ Correct Answer
trees
✗ Wrong Answer
people
💡 Why?
The previous sentence discusses trees as the main subject. “Them” refers back to trees.
(c) “We” refers to —
✓ Correct Answer
human beings / people
✗ Wrong Answer
trees
💡 Why?
People are the ones who should plant and care for trees.
(d) “Them” in “take care of them” refers to —
✓ Correct Answer
trees
✗ Wrong Answer
people
💡 Why?
We take care of trees, so “them” refers to trees.
(e) “Therefore” indicates —
✓ Correct Answer
a result or consequence
✗ Wrong Answer
a question
💡 Why?
“Therefore” introduces a result or conclusion based on what was stated before.
Marking: 1 mark each.
Topic: Modifiers

9. MODIFIERS — 5 Marks

(a) ___, he went to school.
✓ Correct Answer
Being tired, he went to school.
✗ Common Wrong Answer
Being tired, school went to him.
💡 Why?
The introductory participial phrase must logically modify the subject he.
(b) The man ___ is my uncle.
✓ Correct Answer
The man who lives next door is my uncle.
✗ Common Wrong Answer
The man which lives next door is my uncle.
💡 Why?
For people, the relative pronoun who is appropriate.
(c) ___, he went to Dhaka.
✓ Correct Answer
To attend the meeting, he went to Dhaka.
✗ Common Wrong Answer
To attended the meeting, he went to Dhaka.
💡 Why?
An infinitive of purpose uses to + base verb, so “to attend” is correct.
(d) The girl ___ is my sister.
✓ Correct Answer
The girl wearing a blue dress is my sister.
✗ Common Wrong Answer
The girl wear a blue dress is my sister.
💡 Why?
The present participle wearing works as a reduced relative modifier describing the girl.
(e) ___, he completed the work.
✓ Correct Answer
With great care, he completed the work.
✗ Common Wrong Answer
With great carefully, he completed the work.
💡 Why?
“Care” is a noun, so the phrase with great care is grammatically correct.
Important: Other grammatically correct modifiers may also be accepted.
Marking: 1 mark each.
Topic: Sentence Connectors

10. SENTENCE CONNECTORS — 5 Marks

(a) He is poor. ___, he is honest.
✓ Correct Answer
However
✗ Wrong Answer
Therefore
💡 Why?
The two ideas contrast with each other: he is poor, but he is honest.
(b) He worked hard. ___, he failed.
✓ Correct Answer
However / Nevertheless
✗ Wrong Answer
Therefore
💡 Why?
Working hard would normally suggest success, but the second sentence presents a contrasting result.
(c) Study hard, ___ you will fail.
✓ Correct Answer
otherwise
✗ Wrong Answer
because
💡 Why?
“Otherwise” means “if you do not study hard”.
(d) ___ he is rich, he is unhappy.
✓ Correct Answer
Although / Though
✗ Wrong Answer
Because
💡 Why?
Being rich and being unhappy are presented as contrasting ideas.
(e) He could not go out ___ it was raining.
✓ Correct Answer
because
✗ Wrong Answer
although
💡 Why?
The rain is the reason he could not go outside. Therefore, because expresses the correct cause-and-effect relationship.
Marking: 1 mark each.
Topic: Synonyms and Antonyms

11. SYNONYMS AND ANTONYMS — 5 Marks

(a) Brave — Synonym
✓ Correct Answer
courageous
✗ Wrong Answer
cowardly
💡 Why?
“Courageous” means brave. “Cowardly” has the opposite meaning.
(b) Rapid — Synonym
✓ Correct Answer
fast
✗ Wrong Answer
slow
💡 Why?
“Rapid” means happening quickly or at high speed.
(c) Ancient — Synonym
✓ Correct Answer
old
✗ Wrong Answer
modern
💡 Why?
“Ancient” refers to something belonging to a very old time.
(d) Honest — Synonym
✓ Correct Answer
truthful
✗ Wrong Answer
dishonest
💡 Why?
“Truthful” means honest and not telling lies.
(e) Difficult — Synonym
✓ Correct Answer
hard
✗ Wrong Answer
easy
💡 Why?
“Hard” can mean difficult.
(f) Success — Antonym
✓ Correct Answer
failure
✗ Wrong Answer
achievement
💡 Why?
“Failure” is the opposite of success.
(g) Increase — Antonym
✓ Correct Answer
decrease
✗ Wrong Answer
grow
💡 Why?
“Decrease” means to become less, which is opposite to increase.
(h) Strong — Antonym
✓ Correct Answer
weak
✗ Wrong Answer
powerful
💡 Why?
“Weak” is the opposite of strong.
(i) Accept — Antonym
✓ Correct Answer
reject
✗ Wrong Answer
receive
💡 Why?
“Reject” means to refuse or not accept.
(j) Beautiful — Antonym
✓ Correct Answer
ugly
✗ Wrong Answer
attractive
💡 Why?
“Ugly” has the opposite meaning of beautiful.

Topic: Punctuation

12. PUNCTUATION — 5 Marks

Question:

rahim said to karim where are you going karim replied i am going to the market do you need anything
✓ Correct Answer

Rahim said to Karim, “Where are you going?”

Karim replied, “I am going to the market. Do you need anything?”

✗ Incorrect Example
rahim said to karim “where are you going” karim replied i am going to the market do you need anything
💡 Why?
The corrected version needs proper capitalization, quotation marks, commas, question marks and full stops. Direct speech should also be clearly separated from the reporting clauses.
Marking: 5 marks for correct capitalization, quotation marks, commas, question marks and full stops.

Topic: Formal Application

13. FORMAL APPLICATION — 8 Marks

✓ Model Answer

To

The Principal
[Name of College]
[College Address]

Subject: Prayer for increasing library facilities.

Sir/Madam,

I beg most respectfully to state that our college has a library, but its facilities are not sufficient for the students. There are not enough recent books, reference books, newspapers and other educational materials. The number of seats is also insufficient for the students.

A well-equipped library is essential for developing students’ knowledge and encouraging regular study. Therefore, I request you to take necessary steps to increase the number of books, reference materials, newspapers, journals and seating facilities in the library.

I shall be highly obliged if you kindly consider our prayer and take the necessary measures.

Yours obediently,

[Name]
Class: XII
Roll: ______
Date: ______

✗ Common Weak/Wrong Answer
Dear Principal, please give us more books. Our library is not good. Give us books quickly.

This is not a complete formal application because it lacks the proper application format, subject, formal language, organized details and proper closing.
💡 Why the model answer is correct
A formal application should normally include:

• Recipient
• Institution/address
• Subject
• Formal salutation
• Clear purpose
• Relevant reasons/details
• Polite request
• Formal closing
• Name/class/roll/date
Suggested Marking Guide — 8 Marks
  • Proper format — 2
  • Clear subject/purpose — 1
  • Relevant content — 2
  • Organization/coherence — 1
  • Grammar/sentence construction — 1
  • Spelling/punctuation/language — 1
Topic: Report Writing

14. REPORT WRITING — 8 Marks

✓ Model Answer
ROAD ACCIDENT CAUSES INJURIES IN THE CITY

Staff Reporter, Dhaka, 21 September 2026:

A serious road accident occurred in our area yesterday afternoon when a speeding bus collided with a motorcycle near the main intersection. According to local people, the bus was travelling at excessive speed and the driver lost control of the vehicle.

Several people were injured in the accident. The injured were immediately taken to a nearby hospital by local people and emergency responders. The accident caused severe traffic congestion in the area for nearly an hour.

Police arrived at the scene and brought the situation under control. They also began an investigation into the incident. Local residents have demanded stricter enforcement of traffic rules and greater road-safety awareness to prevent such accidents in the future.

✗ Common Weak/Wrong Answer
A bus accident happened yesterday. Many people saw it. It was very bad. Police came. That is all.

This is too short and does not provide sufficient details about the accident, injuries, response, consequences or safety concerns.
Important correction: The headline should match the facts in the report. Since the model report states that people were injured but does not state that anyone died, “causes injuries” is more consistent than “claims lives”.
💡 Why the model answer works
The report includes the main elements expected in a news report: headline, reporter/date/place, incident details, causes, consequences, police response and public reaction.
Marking Guide — 8 Marks
  • Headline — 1
  • Reporter/place/date — 1
  • Relevant facts — 2
  • Causes and consequences — 1
  • Organization/coherence — 1
  • Grammar/vocabulary — 1
  • Spelling/punctuation — 1
Topic: Paragraph Writing

15. PARAGRAPH WRITING — 10 Marks

✓ Model Answer
THE USES AND ABUSES OF SOCIAL MEDIA

Social media has become an important part of modern life. Platforms such as Facebook, YouTube, Instagram and other online services help people communicate with friends and family and share information quickly. Students can also use social media for educational purposes by accessing lectures, tutorials, news and learning materials. Businesses use these platforms for communication, marketing and customer service.

However, social media also has several negative aspects. Excessive use can waste valuable time and reduce concentration on study or work. False information, cyberbullying, online fraud and privacy problems are also serious concerns. Some people become dependent on social media and neglect their real-life responsibilities.

Therefore, social media should be used carefully and responsibly. Proper awareness and self-control can help us enjoy its benefits while avoiding its harmful effects.

✗ Common Weak/Wrong Answer
Social media is very good. We use Facebook and YouTube. Sometimes it is bad. Students should use it. I like social media very much.

This answer is too short, lacks proper development and does not sufficiently discuss both the uses and abuses of social media.
💡 Why the model answer works
The paragraph discusses both sides of the topic:

• Communication
• Education
• Business
• Time wasting
• False information
• Cyberbullying
• Online fraud
• Privacy problems
• Responsible use
Marking Guide — 10 Marks
  • Introduction — 1
  • Relevant content — 4
  • Organization/coherence — 2
  • Grammar/sentence construction — 1
  • Vocabulary — 1
  • Spelling/punctuation — 1
Topic: Composition

16. COMPOSITION — 14 Marks

✓ Model Answer
THE ROLE OF STUDENTS IN BUILDING THE NATION

Students are an important part of a nation because they represent its future. The development of a country depends greatly on the knowledge, skills, values and responsibilities that young people develop during their student life. Therefore, students should prepare themselves properly so that they can contribute positively to society.

The first duty of students is to study sincerely. Education gives them knowledge and develops their ability to think, solve problems and make responsible decisions. Students should also develop discipline, honesty, punctuality and respect for others. These qualities help them become responsible citizens.

Students can contribute to society in many ways. They can participate in voluntary activities, social- awareness campaigns, tree-plantation programmes, blood-donation campaigns and disaster-relief activities. They can also help spread awareness about education, health, environmental protection, road safety and responsible use of technology.

In the modern world, students should develop technological and communication skills. They should use the internet and social media responsibly and avoid spreading false information. They should also respect the laws of the country and promote cooperation and tolerance among people.

Students are not only learners; they are future professionals, researchers, teachers, entrepreneurs, administrators and leaders. If they receive proper education and develop strong moral values, they can play an important role in the progress of the country.

In conclusion, students should use their time effectively, acquire knowledge, develop good character and serve society whenever possible. A responsible and educated student community can make a meaningful contribution to the development of the nation.

✗ Common Weak/Wrong Answer
Students are important. They study in schools and colleges. They should help the country. They should work hard and become good people. Students can build the nation.

The main problem is not necessarily that every sentence is grammatically wrong. The answer is weak because the ideas are not sufficiently developed for a long composition. It lacks examples, organization, explanation and detailed discussion of students’ responsibilities.
💡 Why the model answer works
The composition develops the topic through several clear areas:

• Students as the future of the nation
• Importance of education
• Discipline and moral values
• Social and voluntary activities
• Technology and communication skills
• Responsible citizenship
• Future professional roles
• Contribution to national development
Suggested Marking Guide — 14 Marks
  • Introduction and relevance — 2
  • Development of ideas — 5
  • Organization and coherence — 2
  • Grammar and sentence construction — 2
  • Vocabulary and expression — 1
  • Spelling and punctuation — 2
