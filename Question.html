PART I — READING
Read the passage carefully and answer Questions 1 and 2.

Aisha grew up in a small village where clean drinking water was hard to find. Every morning, she watched her mother walk nearly two kilometres to fetch water from a distant pond. The water was often muddy and unsafe to drink, and many children in the village suffered from waterborne diseases as a result. Aisha was only fourteen when she decided that she wanted to solve this problem.

She began reading about simple filtration methods in old science magazines borrowed from her school library. With the help of her science teacher, she experimented with sand, charcoal, and gravel layered inside a plastic container. After several failed attempts, she finally built a small filter that could make muddy pond water clearer within minutes.

Aisha’s invention did not require electricity or expensive materials, so almost any family in her village could build one. She demonstrated her filter at a school science fair, where it caught the attention of a local NGO. The organisation helped her distribute the design to more than fifty households during the following year.

Because of Aisha’s simple invention, the rate of waterborne illness in her village dropped noticeably within two years. She later said that the biggest lesson she had learned was that a small idea, if pursued with patience, could bring real change to people’s lives. Today, she dreams of studying environmental engineering so that she can help more communities gain access to clean water.

Question 4 — Summary

Bees play a far more important role in our lives than most people realise. Although they are often seen only as producers of honey, their real contribution lies in pollination. As bees move from flower to flower collecting nectar, they transfer pollen grains, which allows plants to reproduce and produce fruits, vegetables, and seeds. Scientists estimate that nearly one-third of the food we eat depends, directly or indirectly, on pollination carried out by bees and other insects.

In recent decades, bee populations across the world have been declining rapidly. Several factors are responsible for this decline, including the heavy use of pesticides in farming, the destruction of natural habitats due to urbanisation, and the effects of climate change on flowering patterns.

When bee populations shrink, crop yields fall, food prices rise, and entire ecosystems become unstable, since many other creatures also depend on the plants that bees help pollinate.

To protect bees, experts recommend several measures. Farmers can reduce the use of harmful pesticides and adopt more natural methods of pest control. Governments can create protected green spaces where wildflowers and bee colonies can thrive. Ordinary citizens can also help simply by planting flowering plants in their gardens or on their balconies.

Write a summary of the passage in about 100 words.

Model Summary

Bees are essential to human life because they play a major role in pollination. By transferring pollen from flower to flower, they help plants reproduce and produce fruits, vegetables and seeds. A significant part of the world's food supply depends on pollination. However, bee populations are declining because of pesticides, habitat destruction and climate change. Their decline can reduce crop production, increase food prices and disturb ecosystems. To protect bees, harmful pesticides should be reduced, natural habitats should be preserved and flowering plants should be grown. Protecting bees is therefore important for food security and the balance of nature.

Question 7 — Rearranging Sentences

Rearrange the following sentences to make a meaningful paragraph.

(a) As a result, he became one of the most respected scientists of his time.

(b) He was born into a poor family in a small village.

(c) Despite many hardships, he continued his studies with determination.

(d) A great scientist devoted his whole life to research.

(e) His discoveries brought a revolutionary change in the field of science.

(f) From childhood, he showed a keen interest in science.

(g) Later, he got the chance to study at a reputed university.

Correct Order

d → b → f → c → g → e → a

Complete paragraph:

A great scientist devoted his whole life to research. He was born into a poor family in a small village. From childhood, he showed a keen interest in science. Despite many hardships, he continued his studies with determination. Later, he got the chance to study at a reputed university. His discoveries brought a revolutionary change in the field of science. As a result, he became one of the most respected scientists of his time.

Question 8 — Data Description

The table below shows the number of internet users in Bangladesh over five years. Study the data and write a description of the trend in about 200 words.

Year Internet Users
2019 50 million
2020 65 million
2021 80 million
2022 100 million
2023 130 million

Model Answer

The table shows the number of internet users in Bangladesh over a period of five years from 2019 to 2023. The figures indicate a continuous upward trend in internet usage during the period.

In 2019, there were 50 million internet users in Bangladesh. The number increased to 65 million in 2020 and then rose to 80 million in 2021. In 2022, the number reached 100 million. Finally, in 2023, the number increased sharply to 130 million.

Overall, the number of internet users increased by 80 million over the five-year period. The largest increase occurred between 2022 and 2023, when the number rose by 30 million. The figures therefore show a steady expansion of internet use in Bangladesh.

The increasing number of users may reflect wider access to smartphones, digital services and internet connections. Internet use has also become increasingly important for communication, education, business and entertainment. Thus, the data clearly demonstrates a significant growth in internet usage between 2019 and 2023.

Question 9 — Story Completion

Complete the following story. Give it a suitable title and moral.

“Once a poor farmer found a bag full of money on the road while returning home from the market…”

Title: Honesty Is the Best Policy

Once a poor farmer found a bag full of money on the road while returning home from the market. He picked it up and looked around, but there was nobody nearby. Although he was very poor and badly needed money, he decided not to keep it. He took the bag home and waited for its owner.

The next morning, the farmer went to the local market and asked people whether anyone had lost a bag of money. After some time, a wealthy merchant came to him. He described the bag correctly and told the farmer that it contained a large amount of money.

The farmer returned the bag without taking anything from it. The merchant was extremely happy and offered him a reward. At first, the farmer refused, saying that he had only done his duty. The merchant was impressed by his honesty and later helped him start a small business.

The farmer's honest decision changed his life. He became respected by everyone in the village.

Moral: Honesty is always rewarded.
