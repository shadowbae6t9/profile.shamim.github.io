The Principal
[Name of College]

Subject: Prayer for increasing library facilities.

Sir/Madam,

I beg most respectfully to state that our college has a library, but its facilities are not sufficient for the students. There are not enough recent books, reference books, newspapers and other educational materials. The number of seats is also insufficient for the students.

A well-equipped library is essential for developing students’ knowledge and encouraging regular study. Therefore, I request you to take necessary steps to increase the number of books, reference materials, newspapers, journals and seating facilities in the library.

I shall be highly obliged if you kindly consider our prayer and take the necessary measures.

14. REPORT WRITING — 8 Marks

ROAD ACCIDENT CLAIMS LIVES IN THE CITY

Staff Reporter, Dhaka, 21 September 2026:

A serious road accident occurred in our area yesterday afternoon when a speeding bus collided with a motorcycle near the main intersection. According to local people, the bus was travelling at excessive speed and the driver lost control of the vehicle.

Several people were injured in the accident. The injured were immediately taken to a nearby hospital by local people and emergency responders. The accident caused severe traffic congestion in the area for nearly an hour.

Police arrived at the scene and brought the situation under control. They also began an investigation into the incident. Local residents have demanded stricter enforcement of traffic rules and greater road-safety awareness to prevent such accidents in the future.

15. PARAGRAPH WRITING — 10 Marks

THE USES AND ABUSES OF SOCIAL MEDIA

Social media has become an important part of modern life. Platforms such as Facebook, YouTube, Instagram and other online services help people communicate with friends and family and share information quickly. Students can also use social media for educational purposes by accessing lectures, tutorials, news and learning materials. Businesses use these platforms for communication, marketing and customer service.

However, social media also has several negative aspects. Excessive use can waste valuable time and reduce concentration on study or work. False information, cyberbullying, online fraud and privacy problems are also serious concerns. Some people become dependent on social media and neglect their real-life responsibilities.

Therefore, social media should be used carefully and responsibly. Proper awareness and self-control can help us enjoy its benefits while avoiding its harmful effects.

Marking Guide — 10 Marks
  • Introduction — 1
  • Relevant content — 4
  • Organization/coherence — 2
  • Grammar/sentence construction — 1
  • Vocabulary — 1
  • Spelling/punctuation — 1

16. COMPOSITION — 14 Marks

THE ROLE OF STUDENTS IN BUILDING THE NATION

Students are an important part of a nation because they represent its future. The development of a country depends greatly on the knowledge, skills, values and responsibilities that young people develop during their student life. Therefore, students should prepare themselves properly so that they can contribute positively to society.

The first duty of students is to study sincerely. Education gives them knowledge and develops their ability to think, solve problems and make responsible decisions. Students should also develop discipline, honesty, punctuality and respect for others. These qualities help them become responsible citizens.

Students can contribute to society in many ways. They can participate in voluntary activities, social-awareness campaigns, tree-plantation programmes, blood-donation campaigns and disaster-relief activities. They can also help spread awareness about education, health, environmental protection, road safety and responsible use of technology.

In the modern world, students should develop technological and communication skills. They should use the internet and social media responsibly and avoid spreading false information. They should also respect the laws of the country and promote cooperation and tolerance among people.

Students are not only learners; they are future professionals, researchers, teachers, entrepreneurs, administrators and leaders. If they receive proper education and develop strong moral values, they can play an important role in the progress of the country.

In conclusion, students should use their time effectively, acquire knowledge, develop good character and serve society whenever possible. A responsible and educated student community can make a meaningful contribution to the development of the nation.
